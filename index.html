// 01 — About

Where institutional
depth meets venture velocity

"The biggest challenge is rarely the technology itself — it's aligning incentives across institutions, founders, and regulators while ensuring systems are secure and operationally viable."

My work has largely focused on bridging two ecosystems that historically move at very different speeds: early-stage emerging technology innovation and large financial institutions. I spent much of my career inside asset management organizations where the priority is protecting client assets and operating within highly regulated environments. At the same time, I have worked closely with founders and venture investors building the next generation of financial infrastructure.

Recognizing the gap between those worlds, I helped build a venture and partnership platform designed to evaluate emerging technologies while connecting founders with large institutional partners. The goal was not simply investing in companies, but helping institutions responsibly adopt innovation across areas like fintech infrastructure, data platforms, blockchain, and AI-driven financial tools.

That experience gave me a unique perspective on how innovation actually scales in financial services. Today I continue to focus on identifying technologies that can reshape financial infrastructure, particularly where AI, data platforms, and institutional workflows intersect.
